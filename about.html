Our Story & Mission

Bridging Timeless Tradition with Clear Preparation

Planning a traditional ceremony often involves coordinating multiple family members, understanding specific Samagri requirements, and finding a priest fluent in the family's ritual tradition and language.

Sacred Sankalpam was conceived to organize these essential details into one calm, transparent space—allowing families to focus on devotion rather than administrative confusion.

Our Mission Make ceremony discovery, Samagri checklists, and priest scheduling clear without guesswork.
Cultural Reverence Highlight ritual significance, expected duration, and regional traditions respectfully.
Guiding Principles

Values Behind Every Ceremony

Every feature on the platform reflects four foundational commitments to devotees, priests, and ceremony organizers.

01

Authenticity

Ceremony summaries respect Vedic customs, regional sampradayas, and traditional preparation guidelines.

02

Clarity

Transparent durations, language details, and Samagri lists help households prepare well in advance.

03

Accountability

Distinct approval and scheduling states ensure families know whether a request is pending or confirmed.

04

Inclusivity

Support for multiple Indian languages and accessible layouts serves both local and global families.

Structured Coordination

Priest Approval & Schedule Integrity

Clear workflow rules protect both families and priests from double-bookings, unverified listings, and ambiguous request statuses.

✓

Admin-Reviewed Priest Profiles

Only priest profiles reviewed and approved by an administrator are intended for public directory visibility and booking selection.

◷

Slot & Date Conflict Prevention

Blocked calendar dates and overlapping active bookings on the same date and time slot are prevented automatically.

▤

Separate Status Visibility

Booking approval status and payment status remain clearly separated so devotees and priests always see accurate progress.

Platform Architecture

Designed for Every Participant

Dedicated portals keep responsibilities organized across devotees, priests, and platform administrators.

01 / Families & Hosts

Devotee Experience

Browse rituals by occasion, compare priest languages and specializations, review Samagri checklists, and track request updates from a single dashboard.

02 / Vedic Practitioners

Priest Workspace

Manage ceremonial specializations, block unavailable dates, review incoming family requests, and confirm schedules with clarity.

03 / Quality Oversight

Administrator Portal

Verify priest credentials prior to listing, maintain accurate pooja catalogs, and oversee booking integrity across the platform.

Platform Craft

Accessible Engineering & Prototype Transparency

Built with lightweight web standards and clear disclosure of current demonstration boundaries.

Accessible, Responsive Web Craft

Built cleanly with semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript—featuring an English-first interface, Light and Dark reading modes, Left-to-Right and Right-to-Left layout toggling, and mobile-ready responsiveness.

Current Frontend Prototype Scope

This application currently operates as an interactive frontend prototype powered by sample records and browser local storage. Backend authentication, persistent cloud databases, payment gateways, and live notifications are reserved for future integration.
