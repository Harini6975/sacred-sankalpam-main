Details to Include in Your Message

Sharing a few key details helps our support team give clearer answers for family ceremonies or portal workflows:

  • Preferred ceremony name (such as Gruhapravesam, Ganapathi Homam, or Marriage Pooja) and city.
  • Target date, time window, and venue type (home, apartment, or hall).
  • Preferred language for mantras and sankalpam explanation.
