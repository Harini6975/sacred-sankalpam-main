Your Preparation Guide

Every Detail Deserves Careful Attention

A well-organized ceremony begins with clear information. Use this preparation guide to keep important details together before sending your booking request.

  • Choose the ceremony: Identify the pooja or family occasion you wish to organize.
  • Confirm the date: Discuss the preferred date and auspicious time with your family and relevant priest.
  • Decide the venue: Prepare the complete home, hall or venue address.
  • Review Samagri: Ask for the applicable ritual materials and preparation list.
  • Share special requirements: Mention language preferences, family customs and other relevant arrangements.

Helpful Information

Questions Before You Begin?

Find answers to common questions about ceremony selection, priest profiles and the booking process.

Need more information about the platform? Visit our contact page to find the available support details.

Contact Support
How do I find a suitable priest?

Visit the Priests page to review available profiles, ceremony specializations and language information. You can then use the booking interface to submit your request.

Are all priest profiles verified?

The intended platform workflow allows administrators to review priest submissions and approve profiles before making them publicly visible. Check the profile status and platform verification details.

Can I request a particular ceremony?

Browse the Services page to find listed ceremonies. For a ceremony that is not listed, contact support and explain your requirements.

Is my booking confirmed immediately?

A booking request is not necessarily a confirmed booking. Confirmation depends on priest availability and the platform's approval workflow.

Can I check Samagri requirements before booking?

Review the ceremony details and any available preparation information. Confirm the final Samagri list with the assigned priest because requirements may differ by ritual and family tradition.

Can I use Sacred Sankalpam on my mobile?

The interface is designed to adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes. You can access the website using a modern mobile browser.

Can families coordinate from outside India?

Families can use the website to explore ceremony information and submit requests. Confirm the ceremony's local date, time zone, venue and priest availability before finalizing arrangements.
