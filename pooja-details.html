    Ritual Sequence

    How the Ceremony Unfolds

    While regional traditions vary, most Vedic ceremonies progress through four harmonious stages.

    01

    Sankalpam & Purification

    Invocation of blessings, lamp lighting, and stating the family's auspicious intention and gothram.

    02

    Kalasha & Vigneshwara Pooja

    Consecrating the sacred water vessel and offering initial prayers to Lord Ganesha for obstacle-free proceedings.

    03

    Pradhana Ritual / Homam

    Core mantra recitation, sacred fire offerings or archana, and observance of the main ceremony.

    04

    Maha Mangala Arati

    Final camphor arati, Vedic blessings from elders and priest, and distribution of sacred prasad.

    Home Readiness

    Preparing Your Ceremony Space

    Ensure a clean, well-ventilated area facing east or north for the altar or homa kundam. Keep seating mats, drinking water, and steel or brass plates ready before the priest arrives.
